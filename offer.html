⚡FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly is one generation?

One finished, brand-ready video concept of your product, up to about 15 seconds, delivered in three cuts (9:16, 1:1, and 16:9) with captions and a hook variation. One generation, three ready-to-post formats.

How real does it look?

That is the whole point. We use our own fine-tuned models and a hand-finished process, not off-the-shelf tools, so the output is built to pass as a real shoot instead of looking AI-generated.

Do I need to know how to prompt or direct?

No, and that is the point. AI tools only look this good in the hands of someone who directs commercials for a living. You send a product link, we do the directing, prompting, and editing. You just get the finished video.

Why not just use an AI video tool myself?

You can, and the tools are genuinely powerful. But a usable result takes a director's eye and a lot of paid retries before anything looks real. We are the done-for-you layer on top: no tools to learn, no credits to burn, no reject fees. You pay once and we hand you the finished cut.

Will it look like everyone else's AI content?

No. Every video is built around your product, your brand, and a custom concept, then hand-finished. The goal is work that looks like a real shoot made for you, not the same generic AI look everyone is posting.

What do you need from me?

A link to your product and any photos or brand colors you already have. If you have a reference vibe you like, send it. If not, we go with our best judgment.

Can I get changes?

Yes. Every paid order includes a free revision round so we can dial it in until it is right.

Do I own the videos?

Yes. You get full usage rights for paid ads and organic content, on every platform.

How fast is delivery?

Within 48 hours of receiving your product details, for both free samples and paid orders.

What if I need a lot of content?

Pick the Scale plan and book a call. We set up an ongoing pipeline with volume pricing and priority delivery.
