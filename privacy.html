Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Hexa AI collects when you use our website and order our video service, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Hexa AI ("we", "us", "our"), operated by Hexa AI Agency, produces hyper-real AI video content for brands. You can reach us any time at hello@madebyhexa.co.

Information we collect

Information you give us

When you request a free sample or place an order, we collect the details you submit through our forms:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your brand or company name and website
  • Product details and links you share with us
  • Any files, assets, notes, or references you upload for your order

Payment information

Payments are processed by Stripe. Your card details go directly to Stripe and are never stored on our servers. We receive only the information needed to confirm and fulfil your order, such as your name, email, and which package you bought.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we automatically receive basic technical data when you visit, such as your IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed, and how you arrived at the site. We use advertising and measurement pixels to understand how our ads perform (see Cookies and tracking below).

How we use your information

  • To deliver your free sample or paid order and communicate with you about it
  • To respond to your questions and provide support
  • To process payments and prevent fraud
  • To measure and improve our marketing, website, and service

Cookies and tracking

We use the Meta (Facebook) Pixel and the TikTok Pixel to measure ad performance and reach people who may be interested in our work. These tools may set cookies and share limited event data (such as page views, leads, and purchases) with Meta and TikTok.

You can limit this tracking by adjusting your browser cookie settings and your ad preferences on Meta and TikTok.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run our business:

  • Stripe for payment processing
  • Netlify for website hosting and form submissions
  • Zoho Mail for sending and receiving email
  • Meta and TikTok for advertising measurement

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

How long we keep it

We keep order and contact information for as long as needed to deliver your work, provide support, and meet legal and accounting obligations. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, and we will do so unless we are required to keep it.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. To make a request, email hello@madebyhexa.co and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Governing law

This policy and our handling of your information are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, United States. The entity responsible for your information is Hexa AI Agency.

Contact
