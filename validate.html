How we decide

Time is the only vote we count

Competitor ads are weighted by how long they have been running, so an ad live for 200 days counts 200 times one that lasted a day. A brand can buy a launch. It cannot buy six months of a losing ad.

Nothing is asserted without its source

Every quote carries a link to where it was said. If a finding cannot be traced back to something a real person wrote, it does not appear on the page at all.

We tell you when the answer is no

If the read says your category is won by still images, it says so, even though video is the more expensive thing we sell. A read you cannot trust against us is worth nothing to you.
