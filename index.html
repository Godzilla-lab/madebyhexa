Pass 01 / 03
  1. 01

    We read your market

    We start with your product page, then look outward.

    What are buyers saying? The conversations, complaints, questions and patterns around your category, written by people who had no idea a brand was listening.

    What is already working? Every ad running in your market, and how long each one has survived. An ad still running after six months is proven. One that lasted four days was somebody's guess.

    What buyers care about + what competitors have proven = where the opportunity is

    What your buyers say. What already works.
  2. 02

    We find the angle

    We identify the strongest thing your market is saying that competitors are not properly answering, ranked by how many separate people raised it, every one with its receipts attached.

    Then we decide how to say it, static or video, based on what is actually surviving in your category. If your market is won by still images we say so, even though video is the more expensive thing we sell.

    You do not have to choose the format, write the hook, or come up with the concept. The research makes the call.

    What to say. And how to say it.
  3. Everything above · free, no account
  4. 03

    We make the ad

    Same product, same evidence, no brief for you to write.

    The hook comes from the finding. The concept comes from the angle. The creative is built around the argument, so the ad argues something your market has already said it cares about.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does one ad cost?

Every format has one clear price, from for a scroll-stopping ad to for a premium one. A 15-second video is the base; each extra 15 seconds adds . The exact total shows before you pay, always.

How real does it look?

That is the whole point. If an ad looks AI generated it does not ship, so what you get is built to pass as a real shoot.

What is the read, and what does it cost?

It is the research pass that decides what your ad should say: what real buyers in your category complain about, which competitor ads have run long enough to count as proven, and whether your category is won by video or by statics. It is free, and you do not need an account to see the first findings.

Where does the research come from?

Public sources only. The community threads where your category gets argued about, and the public ad libraries where every advertiser's live ads and start dates are already on the record. Every quote on your report links to the thread it came from, so you can check any claim yourself.

Do I need to know how to prompt?

No, and you do not need to know what to say either. The read hands the studio the angle and the opening line, so the brief is written before you touch anything.

How fast is delivery?

Most orders finish in minutes and you can watch them build live on the order page. Longer ads take a little more.

Can I get longer than 15 seconds?

Yes, up to 30 seconds as one continuous film: same creator, same scene, one storyboard, so it cuts together like a single take.

Do I own the content?

Yes. You get full usage rights for paid ads and organic content, on every platform.

What if something goes wrong?

You are never charged for work we do not deliver, and our refund policy covers the rest. Reply to any order email and we make it right.

No product link?

No problem. Start with photos or a plain-language description and we build from that.
