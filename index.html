FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does one video cost?

Every format has one clear price, from $12 for short-form motion to $29 for a premium spot. A 15-second video is the base; each extra 15 seconds adds $12. The exact total shows before you pay, always.

How real does it look?

That is the whole point. Every order runs through our own tuned pipeline, so the output is built to pass as a real shoot instead of looking AI-generated.

Do I need to know how to prompt?

No. Pick a format and a look; the brief is already written and tested. You add your product, everything else is handled.

How fast is delivery?

Most orders finish in minutes and you can watch them build live on the order page. Longer films take a little more.

Can I get longer than 15 seconds?

Yes, up to 2 minutes. Longer runs are delivered as one continuous film: same creator, same scene, one storyboard, so it cuts together like a single take.

Do I own the content?

Yes. You get full usage rights for paid ads and organic content, on every platform.

What if something goes wrong?

You are never charged for work we do not deliver, and our refund policy covers the rest. Reply to any order email and we make it right.

No product link?

No problem. Start with photos or a plain-language description and we build from that.
